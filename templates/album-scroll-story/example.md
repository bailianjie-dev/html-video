# Scroll Story Album example

Create an electronic album for Aoki Company.

Title: Aoki Company Album
Subtitle: A compact story about people, products, milestones, and future plans.

Pages:
1. Origin - Built around dependable delivery. Aoki began with one promise: make complex work feel predictable.
2. Team - Practical operators with product taste. Put the workspace or team photo here if uploaded.
3. Product - Clear service, visible progress. Show the product screenshot or material preview if available.
4. Proof - Clients come back for the calm execution. Use a metric, quote, or short timeline.
5. Next - A focused year ahead. End with the next milestone and contact line.

Interaction:
- On mobile, users scroll down to read the next page.
- On desktop, users click Next/Previous or use arrow keys.
